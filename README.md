# ares_thermus
Scripts and files reelvant for the publication Wong et al. 2025, Engineering Artificial 5′ Regulatory Sequences for Thermostable Protein Expression in the Extremophile Thermus thermophilus.

- The notebook is used for data analysis and generating the figures.

- The data folder includes the relevant data.

- The supplementary spreadsheet includes the three supplementary tables cited in the paper.

- The plasmid maps folder includes all the relevant plasmids.
